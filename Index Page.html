IT ACADEMY Portal

Student Management & Attendance System

Streamline your institutional attendance tracking with our high-speed QR code system. Fast, accurate, and secure. Features include real-time check-in and check-out tracking, student registration management, automated ID card generation, and comprehensive reporting tailored for educational institutes and private academies.

Key Capabilities

Quick Scanning

Generate unique, tamper-proof QR codes for each registered student and scan them instantly using any device camera.

Real-time Tracking

Monitor student arrivals and departures in real-time with automatic timestamp logging and immediate visual feedback.

Detailed Reports

Generate institutional attendance reports, filter student logs by name or date, and maintain accurate records effortlessly.
